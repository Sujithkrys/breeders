## 2. India-specific references (our edge)

- **#32 CRED, "the evolution of money"**: sage/cream paper, real rupee notes and coins, a Mumbai billboard with a live counter, a "masala chai" IV-bag joke, a ₹1,000 thali, then tap-to-pay. Witty, warm, unmistakably Indian.
- **#39 UPI revolution**: dark teal world, a glowing orange orb travels the film ("They said… Digital India is just a dream"), a crowd of silhouettes with glowing phones, 3D Send-Money cards on a grid floor, arcs across an India map, and an embossed white UPI logo at the end.
- **I01–I08 postcard edition**: city photo next to a cream paper strip with a condensed serif name, a linocut/riso illustration of the same scene, and a typewriter caption ("No. 041 · Skyline | Coastline | Timeless · 2026").
- **#37 Hindi type motion**: Devanagari kinetic lyrics, spiral text and ink textures. Indian-script motion stands out.
- **#55 Telugu captions, #78 Tamil/Malayalam wedding auto-rickshaw, #16 auto-rickshaw "Moving Metry" exploded panels, #17 Bharatanatyam/Kali poster, #41 Tamil village kite story.**
- Takeaway: Indian everyday objects (auto, rupee, chai, thali, UPI QR, local scripts) told with premium craft is a lane almost nobody in the pile does at launch-film quality.

## 3. Premium styles we can build fully in code

1. **Swiss type specimen** (#73 OpenAI design guidelines): pure white, giant black glyphs, construction lines, outline→solid, dot-matrix circle morph. Cheap to build, looks expensive.
2. **Exploded technical view** (#90 record player): an isometric line drawing splits into labelled assemblies, a list highlights each part, mono caption with real numbers, reassembles and spins. Perfect for explaining a product's insides.
3. **Agent-as-machine** (#94): an isometric wireframe factory where each Claude Code turn lights a station; mono status line. A ready concept for any AI-agent launch.
4. **One-colour editorial** (#80 Ma, #38 Replit): one accent colour, tiny centred text, lots of air, a few cut-outs, grain. Calm and confident.
5. **Real-UI micro-interaction cards** (#89, #91, #92 context window, #93 Hairline): one card, a live number counting, a hover tooltip, blue Figma-style selection boxes with size labels.
6. **Dot-matrix / LED type** (#08 FYD): matches our Apple Pay dot world.
7. **Strip-window collage** (I09, I12–I16): subject cut out on white, with a strip of the original background crossing behind. One mask, very striking.
8. **Brand-coloured physics logo build** (#47 Crumble, made with Opus 5.5 in code; #54 Hello Kay): drop → bounce → morph → word wall → logo.

## 4. Story structures worth stealing

- **Wispr Flow (#97), the best one here.** It opens late at night with an email still being typed. Then the stat: "You think at 220 words a minute. You type at 45." The magic: spoken words spiral out of a mic pill. The theme switches from dark to cream for the use-cases ("Replied.", code, "Happy birthday Mom!", "Inbox zero."). Then the payoff, "Get your night back.", and the logo with "Don't type, just speak." Each beat is one line of type and one UI moment.
- **ServiceUp (#84)**: "Well…" → the pain in money (−$450, Downtime) → "Let's dive into the platform" → agent cards and a map → a word list scrolling through every step → a 30% chart → logo.
- **CRED (#32)**: history → absurd modern comparisons → "The evolution" (cash → QR) → B&W crowd with big words → product.
- **Replit (#38)**: a personal story told in one colour with props (CRT, dice, podium), ending on a question: "What's stopping you?"

## 7. The competition

Many reels in the pile are "Claude/Opus 5.5 made this motion design" ads for skills and MCPs (#02, #04, #19, #25, #24, #47, #48, #52, #64 HookMaster, #74, #88). HookMaster (#64) does almost exactly what we do: Remotion-style launch films from a coding agent. So "made with Claude" alone is no longer novel. What still sets us apart is Indian subjects, real-looking UI, and a story with a payoff line.
